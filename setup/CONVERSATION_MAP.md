# Conversation map

Organize discussions into a handful of subject-oriented conversations; don't start 616 independent chats.

- `00 — Course HQ`: logistics, learning-state decisions, progress and review scheduling.
- `M01 — Orientation & AI Foundations`: AI taxonomy, baseline studies, reproducibility.
- `M02 — Linear Algebra`, `M03 — Calculus & Optimization`, `M04 — Probability & Statistics`, `M05 — Classical Machine Learning`.
- `M06 — Neural Networks & PyTorch`, `M07 — Computer Vision`, `M08 — NLP Foundations`.
- `M09 — Transformers & LLMs`, `M10 — Training Systems`, `M11 — Fine-Tuning & Alignment`.
- `M12 — Retrieval & RAG`, `M13 — Agents & MCP`, `M14 — Generative & Multimodal AI`.
- `M15 — Reinforcement Learning & Beyond`, `M16 — MLOps & Model Serving`.
- `M17 — Evaluation`, `M18 — Security & Governance`, `M19 — Research & Capstones`.

Open module conversations as needed. A complicated module can have more than one chat; link them through a short handover committed to `learning/handovers/`. The learning state, not the chat title, is authoritative.

Chat prompts that work well:

- `Resume our current module using the verified learning state and review findings.`
- `Teach the mathematical intuition first, then derive the formal version.`
- `Give me a hint, not the complete code.`
- `Compare this implementation with a reference model and explain any discrepancy.`
- `Review my pull request [URL]. Identify critical issues and an achievable remediation exercise.`
- `Generate a concise, evidence-tagged handoff for my approval.`
